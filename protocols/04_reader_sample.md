# Reader-judged sample

Status: pre-specified. Fixed on 3 September 2026, before any judgment was entered.

Governs the reader-confirmed units reused in Section 5 and Appendix H.

## Population

7,767 Hindi and Nepali units in twelve cells: two languages crossed with six sources.

- Wikipedia introductory extracts.
- Sentences from Wikipedia extracts of 400 or more characters.
- FLEURS transcripts.
- IndicVoices read-speech transcripts.
- IndicVoices extempore transcripts.
- IndicVoices conversation transcripts.

Every unit has at least 40 characters. The language label is the source's provenance.

## Draw

150 units per cell, without replacement, seed 20260903. The first 50 per cell form stage one, 600 units in all. The remaining 100 per cell form stage two, drawn at the same time.

## Presentation

Rows are shuffled across cells and languages. The reader sees only the text, a judgment column and a notes column. The proposed language, source and cell are not shown.

The five choices are Nepali, Hindi, Both or code-switched, Other language, and Cannot tell.

Readers are asked not to use a language identifier, a translation tool or an AI assistant.

## Readers

Reader 1 judges stage one. Reader 2 judges a 200-unit overlap drawn from stage one, presented in a different order. The key is held separately from both readers' sheets.

## Agreement

Agreement with the provenance label is computed over the units a reader marks Nepali or Hindi. Code-switched, cannot-tell and blank answers are reported separately. Answers are not corrected after they are read.

## Stage-two rule

Stage two is run only if at least one of the following holds on stage one:

- overall agreement is below 0.95;
- any cell's agreement is below 0.90;
- any cell has more than 10% cannot-tell answers;
- inter-reader agreement on the overlap is below 0.90.

## Outcome

Reader 1 answered 593 of 600 units. Agreement was 564 of 569 (0.991). The lowest cell was 0.960. There were 20 code-switched answers and 4 cannot-tell answers.

Reader 2 answered all 200 overlap units. Inter-reader agreement was 183 of 198 (0.924), counting a one-letter answer as a disagreement.

No stage-two condition held, so stage two was not run. The analyses in the paper use the 600 stage-one units.
